Laravel · WordPress · AWS · Laravel Forge · Redis · Horizon · Stripe · Better Stack · Flare

Context

Production development and operational support across a portfolio of client websites and applications. The work spans feature development, integrations, infrastructure, monitoring, and troubleshooting.

My contribution

  • Develop and maintain production applications using Laravel and WordPress.
  • Work with AWS and Laravel Forge deployment infrastructure.
  • Implement and maintain payment-provider integrations, including Stripe-related workflows.

Engineering considerations

Production ownership means more than shipping features. It includes understanding failure modes, monitoring important services, investigating incidents, and making systems easier to operate over time.

Outcome: Contribute to the reliability, maintenance, and ongoing development of a portfolio of production web applications.

The agency portfolio includes 40+ production websites; this is an agency-level figure, not a claim that I personally built all of them.
