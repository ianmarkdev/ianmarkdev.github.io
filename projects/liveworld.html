Social media integrations · Feb 2020 – Mar 2022

LiveWorld

Ruby on Rails · Sidekiq · Angular · PostgreSQL · AWS

Context

LiveWorld's products help brands monitor and engage with their social media audiences, which depends on reliably pulling feeds, posts, and comments from several platforms.

My contribution

  • Integrated the Facebook, Instagram, X, LinkedIn, and TikTok APIs to retrieve feeds, posts, and comments, aggregating data from several hundred social accounts.
  • Contributed to API reliability and retry improvements.
  • Developed customizable Angular dashboard components for client engagement activity.
  • Worked on social publishing, supporting 25–40 active client accounts or social pages across two products.
  • Participated in on-call support, regression testing, and deployment war rooms.

Outcome: The reliability and retry work was associated with a 50–70% reduction in failed feed pulls.
