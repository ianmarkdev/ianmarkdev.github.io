CanvasPrints.com

Ruby on Rails · Shopify GraphQL · ImageMagick · Sidekiq · Bubble.io

Context

An online print business needed its Shopify Plus storefront connected to image processing and production-floor management, so that orders could move from purchase to print with less manual handling.

My contribution

  • Built a private Shopify Plus application connecting the storefront to ImageMagick image processing and Bubble.io floor management.
  • Retrieved customer images and order-specific requirements from Shopify via its GraphQL API.
  • Updated order-item statuses in Bubble.io and ran background jobs with Sidekiq.
  • Migrated the application from Rails 6 to Rails 7.
  • Managed CI/CD and production delivery.

Scale

The application processed 8,000–12,000 orders and 40,000–60,000 images annually.

Outcome: Reduced manual floor-management and status-update work by 30–40%.
